Sources:

Placement evidence: `meiji-placement.json`. Coordinates are WGS84; local x follows the lintel, y is up and z is the pass-through direction before the clockwise heading. Natural terrain is unchanged. The builder selects one surveyed fixed datum from raw ground at the structural supports, keeps the connected upper gate rigid, and extends only buried footing toes to 0.5m below the measured local terrain. Approach roads remain enabled. No reference images, external meshes or glyph files are included.
