Pale stone triple construction and shimenawa are visual interpretation. Overall8.3x5.8m dimensions are estimated; mapped17m gate line is a placement guide rather than a measured architectural span. Placement evidence: `mitsumine-placement.json`. Coordinates are WGS84; local x follows the lintel, y is up and z is the pass-through direction before the clockwise heading. Natural terrain is unchanged. The builder selects one surveyed fixed datum from raw ground at the structural supports, keeps the connected upper gate rigid, and extends only buried footing toes to 0.5m below the measured local terrain. Approach roads remain enabled. No reference images, external meshes or glyph files are included.

The frozen raw survey varies by 3.774m between the gate feet. The authored component shift is +1.10m above the surveyed center datum. This leaves about 2.07m clearance below the uphill auxiliary crossbar through its 0.6m walking band (local x=2.55–3.15m), while embedding the existing uphill lower shaft by about 0.73m. The central passage remains clear. The connected lintels remain rigid; only the buried toes follow the natural slope.
