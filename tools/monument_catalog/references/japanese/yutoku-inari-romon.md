# yutoku-inari-romon

Shrine and city identify polychrome lacquered gate.15.8m width and12m depth approximate OSM roof footprint;15.5m height, bay proportions and ornamental relief estimated from photographs. Dark weathered metal roof, vermilion structure, ochre upper panels, gold rosettes and blue/green/ochre bracket frieze kept as separate materials and geometry. Fine representational carving simplified.

Sources:

- https://yutokusan.jp/en/about/

Placement evidence: `yutoku-placement.json`. Coordinates are WGS84; local x follows the lintel, y is up and z is the pass-through direction before the clockwise heading. Natural terrain is unchanged. The builder selects one surveyed fixed datum from raw ground at the structural supports, keeps the connected upper gate rigid, and extends only buried footing toes to 0.5m below the measured local terrain. Approach roads remain enabled. No reference images, external meshes or glyph files are included.
