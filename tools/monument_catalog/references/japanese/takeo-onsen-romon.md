# takeo-onsen-romon

Official tourism authority identifies Tatsuno Kingo1915 Tenpyo-style vermilion gate;12.5m height and12m main width are estimated architectural proportions. OSM gate footprint includes a narrow rear appendage: anchor and heading use central four roof corners only, not the appendage centroid. Distinct white plaster rounded-corner ground portal, rectangular upper windows, vermilion balcony and gray tile roof with shachi ridge ornaments. This is the historic hot-spring gateway, not a shrine gate.

Sources:

- https://www.takeo-kk.net/sightseeing/001373.php
- https://www.city.takeo.lg.jp/uploads/2021takeosougoupanfu.pdf

Placement evidence: `takeo-placement.json`. Coordinates are WGS84; local x follows the lintel, y is up and z is the pass-through direction before the clockwise heading. Natural terrain is unchanged. The builder selects one surveyed fixed datum from raw ground at the structural supports, keeps the connected upper gate rigid, and extends only buried footing toes to 0.5m below the measured local terrain. Approach roads remain enabled. No reference images, external meshes or glyph files are included.
