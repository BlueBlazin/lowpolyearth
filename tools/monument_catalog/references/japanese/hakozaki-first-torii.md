# hakozaki-first-torii

Study lists4.750m height and0.979m shaft measure; shrine identifies three-piece shafts, unified kasagi/shimagi, and equally long nuki and lintel. Dark weathered Takashima stone follows Fukuoka city cultural record. Width6.3m and4.224m centers are model interpretation, not a survey. First torii node at inner approach, heading perpendicular to OSM path. This is not the concrete great gate demolished in2018.

Sources:

- https://hakozakigu.or.jp/keidai/
- https://ritsumei.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/9377/files/dmuch_13_nakajima2.pdf

Placement evidence: `hakozaki-placement.json`. Coordinates are WGS84; local x follows the lintel, y is up and z is the pass-through direction before the clockwise heading. Natural terrain is unchanged. The builder selects one surveyed fixed datum from raw ground at the structural supports, keeps the connected upper gate rigid, and extends only buried footing toes to 0.5m below the measured local terrain. Approach roads remain enabled. No reference images, external meshes or glyph files are included.
