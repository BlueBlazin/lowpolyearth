Getting started

The controls list every key and trackpad gesture. Settings opens with ⌘ + comma.

Report a problem

Include your app version, macOS version and Mac model. Describe what you expected, what happened and the steps to reproduce it.

Screenshots and logs are optional. Check them before sending: they can contain locations and local file paths. Leave out passwords and other private information.

Privacy questions

Use the same address for questions about your information or to ask for support emails to be deleted. The privacy policy explains how support messages are handled.
